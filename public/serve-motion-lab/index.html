Toss window — the 10/10 lever

eyes → top of head. release low = ball out front = your flat push.

One thought"release between eyes and hairline"

The release window is "between the eyes and the top of the head… less than 6 inches of variability." Release too low → "it gets out in front of you and you're chasing" → square-face flat push. Straight arm; a bent elbow re-adds the variability. Contact quality gates everything: "130 vs 138 on tiny contact differences."
