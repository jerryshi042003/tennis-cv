Approach — edge first

The face stays thin to the path. "A thick strike = little spin… the skill is how thin you can contact and still send it." Squared face here = your flat serve.

Hit — off-center, upper-right

Strike the ball's upper-right, "at the index finger," and not the middle of the strings — "slightly off-center, edge leaning in." Dead middle + square face = slice won't take.

Hook — 2–3 strings carry it

"Hook it on two or three strings and it naturally grips and goes." This is the FEEL you're missing — the ball rides the bed for a beat. 当たって→引っかかって→離れていく.

Release → rotation AFTER

"The ball must contact BEFORE the rotation. Use the rotation first and it's flat." Pronation is the exhaust, not the engine — watch it fire only after the ball leaves.

Finish — ignore it

"The finish doesn't really matter — ball's gone." It only diagnoses: out-to-the-right and unwrapped = you held side-on. Kick note: contact at full extension = hitting down = no kick; keep the shape diagonal, tilt the body.
